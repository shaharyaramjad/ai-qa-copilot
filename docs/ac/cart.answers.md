# Answers to the AC review questions: Cart

Only facts checked on the live site go here (each is confirmed by a passing test).

- Same product twice: after "Add to cart" the button changes to "Remove", so a product cannot be added twice from the product list. (TC-CART-03)
- Product data: Sauce Labs Backpack costs $29.99. Sauce Labs Bike Light costs $9.99. (TC-CART-05, TC-CHK-05)
- With two different products added, the cart icon shows 2. (TC-CART-02)
