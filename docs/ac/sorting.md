# AC: Product sorting

Target: https://www.saucedemo.com (log in as `standard_user` / `secret_sauce`)

- AC-SORT-1: The product list page has a sort dropdown.
- AC-SORT-2: By default the products are sorted by name, A to Z.
- AC-SORT-3: "Name (Z to A)" lists the products in reverse alphabetical order.
- AC-SORT-4: "Price (low to high)" lists the cheapest product first.
- AC-SORT-5: "Price (high to low)" lists the most expensive product first.
- AC-SORT-6: Sorting only changes the order. The same products are still listed.
